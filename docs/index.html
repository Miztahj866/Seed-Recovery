How it works

Every word comes from a fixed list of 2,048. So a computer can try them all.

  1. Unplug, then enter what you have

    Download the app, turn off your internet, and type the words you're sure of. Mark each gap with a ?. Smudged words can use the letters you can still read.

  2. Let it search

    The app tries every possible word in each gap. Built-in math in your phrase (the checksum) throws out most wrong answers in a fraction of a second.

  3. Match it to your wallet

    Many phrases pass the checksum, but only one is yours. Give the app one address from your wallet and it picks out the phrase that owns it.

Why you can trust it

Don't take our word for it. Check.

Nothing leaves your computer

The app has no network access switched on at all. Unplug your internet first anyway; it works the same.

All the code is public

Anyone can read every line on GitHub. The command-line version is free and open source too, under the MIT license.

Checked against the standard

The core math is tested against the official BIP39 test vectors, the same ones other wallet software uses. Run python3 tests/test_vectors.py to see for yourself.

Nobody asks for your words

There's no support chat and no website to type them into. If anyone ever asks for your words, it isn't us.

Never type your seed phrase into a website, including any that claims to be this tool. Recovery only belongs on your own offline computer. Anyone who promises "guaranteed" recovery, or asks for your words or an upfront fee, is running a scam.
